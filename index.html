Sorting Guidelines by Electronic Type

Understand proper handling for different electronics to protect the environment and our collection staff in Telangana.

High Recovery • 160 pts/kg

Smartphones & Tablets

  • Data Erase: Go to Settings ➔ System ➔ Reset ➔ Erase All Data (Factory Reset).
  • Eject Cards: Use a SIM ejector tool to extract nano-SIMs and microSD cards.
  • Screen Integrity: If screen is shattered, place phone in a clear ziplock pouch to prevent cuts.
Never puncture swollen phone batteries; handle with utmost care.
Valuable Circuitry • 140 pts/kg

Laptops & Desktops

  • Drive Sanitation: Back up documents to cloud, then wipe drive using built-in Windows/macOS utilities.
  • Separate Battery: If battery is removable, detach it and place beside the laptop.
  • Include Power Brick: Bundling original chargers increases total recyclable weight & points!
Drives are degaussed and shredded at certified Telangana recycling facilities.
Hazardous Grade • 190 pts/kg

Batteries & Power Banks

  • Tape Terminals: Cover gold/metal contacts with non-conductive tape to prevent sparks.
  • Keep Dry: Never expose discarded lithium packs to water, rain, or high heat.
  • No Metal Mixing: Do not store loose batteries in bags with metal keys or coins.
High points bonus: Diverting batteries prevents groundwater toxic pollution.
Copper & Circuitry • 110 pts/kg

Cables, Adapters & PCBs

  • Detangle Wires: Untangle knots to help recycling sorters quickly categorize copper grades.
  • Coil Neatly: Use rubber bands or bread ties to wrap cables into compact loops.
  • PCBs: Keep motherboards and RAM sticks dry and free of excessive food/drink stains.
Copper and gold traces in cables are 100% recyclable indefinitely!
Heavy Display • 85 pts/kg

Monitors & Television Screens

  • Protect Display Glass: Cover the screen face with a piece of cardboard during transport.
  • Keep Stand Attached: Heavy metal/plastic monitor stands add to your rewarded weight.
  • Remote Controls: Remove standard AA/AAA batteries before submitting remotes.
Old CRT and CCFL screens contain phosphor and mercury that require specialized recovery.
Bulk Volume • 60 pts/kg

Home Electronics & Appliances

  • Drain Fluids: Ensure microwaves, coffee makers, and water purifiers are completely empty and dry.
  • Secure Moving Parts: Tape doors or lids shut so they don't swing open during transit.
  • Clean Surface: Wipe away heavy food residues or dust before taking the submission photo.
Bulk multipliers apply! Larger appliances over 10 kg qualify for +50% Gold Champion points.
